Steel Structural Design

Monorail Beam Design

Analysis and design of underhung monorail beams per AISC 9th Edition ASD and CMAA Specification No. 74 (2004).

⤢

Key features

  • <b>Multi-section support</b>: W, H (built-up), I, UB/UC, HE/IPE
  • Bending including <b>warping stress</b>
  • <b>Bottom-flange checks per CMAA No. 74</b>
  • Includes the <b>overhang</b> (Lo, Lbo, Cbo)
  • Includes vertical impact and horizontal load factors
  • Deflection check Δmax
  • Save/open project
