What we design

Structure types we support

From steel and reinforced-concrete structures to equipment foundations and special composite structures — here is what we can help you design.

01

Steel Structures

  • Shelter — pre-engineered & industrial buildings, incl. overhead-crane buildings; the most common type.
  • Equipment Supporting Shelter — buildings housing heavy equipment with overhead crane (Turbine Hall, HRSG).
  • Equipment Supporting Structure — outdoor steel structures supporting equipment.
  • Pipe Rack — steel racks carrying piping in petrochemical, power and industrial projects.
02

Reinforced Concrete

  • RC buildings — offices and administration buildings.
  • Foundations — footing and foundation systems for buildings.
  • Underground structures — RC tanks and pits.
03

Equipment Foundations

  • Static — vertical vessels, horizontal exchangers.
  • Dynamic — pumps, air compressors (block), turbines GT/ST/Exhaust (with vibration evaluation).
  • Conveyor — foundations for conveyor systems.
04

Special Structures

  • Stacks / chimneys — RC structures or steel derrick towers.
  • Steel–concrete composite — waste bunkers (deep RC pit + steel shelter with overhead crane); steel columns fixed into RC columns (Waste Bunker, Turbine Building).
