Crane Girder Design

Design of crane runway girders: vertical, lateral and longitudinal crane load combinations, biaxial bending with torsion, stability and serviceability deflection checks.

⤢

Key features

  • Crane load combinations: vertical loads with impact, lateral surge and longitudinal tractive forces
  • Biaxial bending checks including runway-girder torsion effects
  • Overall stability and deflection checks against crane serviceability limits
  • Exports a calculation report
  • SI units (kN, m, mm)
